# The Tears of the Sea (unofficial concept)

A concept site for Mabisyo's beat tape [The Tears of the Sea](https://mabisyo.bandcamp.com/album/the-tears-of-the-sea): the cover art brought to life as a seamless underwater loop. An experiment by [Joon Park](https://joonwoopark.me).

Not affiliated with or endorsed by Mabisyo. The music and the artwork belong to their creators.

Static page, no build step. Preview locally with `python3 pipeline/range_server.py 8767`.
